Full process

From paper reel to finished carton.

Every Varma Papers box follows one controlled route: corrugation, cutting, printing, pasting, creasing, slotting and stitching. Here is the complete manufacturing process, step by step.

1
Single face paper corrugation machine

Paper corrugation

Kraft paper is fluted and bonded into single-face corrugated board, in vertical or oblique type.

2
Sheet cutting machine

Sheet cutting

The corrugated roll is cut into sheets of the required size.

3
Board cutting machine

Board cutting

Sheets are trimmed into clean, accurate board for the box design.

4
Sheet pasting machine

Sheet pasting

Layers are pasted together to build strong 3-ply and 5-ply board.

5
Sheet pressing machine

Sheet pressing

Pasted sheets are pressed so the layers bond firmly and stay flat.

6
4-bar rotary cutting and creasing machine

Cutting & creasing

Board is cut and creased to shape, ready for folding.

7
Eccentric slitter

Slitting

Slotting and slitting give clean edges and precise folds.

8
Stitching machine

Stitching

Box joints are stitched to complete the carton.

✓
Finished product

Corrugated box

A strong, consistent carton, quality-checked and ready for your product.
